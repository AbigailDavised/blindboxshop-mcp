## 🔧 Available MCP Tools

### `lookup_blindbox_weight_and_shake_clues`
Look up sealed box gram weights, +/- variance tolerance, center of gravity, shake acoustics, and Secret Chase edition weight anomalies across Pop Mart Hirono, Labubu, Skullpanda, Dimoo, Crybaby, and Sonny Angel.

### `verify_labubu_factory_authenticity`
Verify genuine Pop Mart Labubu V1 Tasty Macarons and V2 Have a Seat factory production batch stamps (Shenzhen, Huizhou, Zhongshan), keyring engraving, inner foil bag seal type, and fabric vs paper tag markers.

### `calculate_blindbox_chase_odds`
Calculate exact cumulative probability of pulling a Secret Chase figure (1:72, 1:144, or 1:288), expected monetary spend, and sealed whole-case advantage based on number of boxes opened.

### `optimize_blindbox_collection_cost`
Determine the mathematically optimal acquisition strategy (blind single pulls vs factory-sealed full case vs buying opened desired figures) given remaining uncollected figures in a series.

### `check_blindbox_anti_counterfeit_features`
Retrieve brand-specific physical inspection points (9-tooth tooth count, matte vinyl blush, UV foot stamp, official hologram QR verification) to spot high-grade counterfeit "Lafufu" replicas.

### `list_popular_blindbox_series_specs`
List standard case configurations, box counts per sealed display case, Secret chase ratios, and official release references for trending designer blind boxes.
